# K-12 OPERATIONAL BUDGET & PROPOSAL PITCHING TEMPLATE
### (Student Project Funding Proposal • Target Budget: 15,000,000 to 19,500,000 VND / ~600 to 800 USD)
* **Core Pilot Project:** VIN BEYOND AI (Student Pioneer Club)
* **Sponsoring Institution:** Vinschool Grand Park High School, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
* **Faculty Advisor:** M.Ed. Pham Ba Hong Son (Mike Pham), Google for Education Certified Innovator

---

## 🎯 I. RATIONALE & OPERATIONAL OBJECTIVES

1. **Policy & Pedagogical Foundation:**
   * Aligned with the UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) and national guidelines on digital competence development and governed AI adoption in secondary schools.
   * Promotes student agency, hands-on STEM project learning, and institutional academic integrity.
2. **Funding Objectives:**
   * Empower students to independently plan, govern, and execute major school initiatives:
     * Organize a 4-session peer-to-peer AI safety workshop series led by the student core team for the wider student body.
     * Host the School-Wide Safe AI Hackathon bringing together 80–100 students (20 teams) alongside teachers and parent observers.
     * Package all learning resources into free Open Educational Resources (OER) for transfer to partner schools from 2027 onward.
