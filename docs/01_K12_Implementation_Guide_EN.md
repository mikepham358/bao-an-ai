# K-12 SCHOOL IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE
### For School Leaders, Faculty Advisors, and Student AI Leadership Clubs

This document provides a comprehensive, step-by-step roadmap to assist any secondary school in adopting and facilitating the **Student-Led AI Safety Competency Framework**.

The framework delegates full operational agency to students: from formulating micro-grant budget proposals to leading peer-to-peer training and running a school-wide Safe AI Appathon.

---

## 🗺️ THE 4-STAGE ACTION ROADMAP

### 🔹 STAGE 1: School Pitching & AI Safety Challenge Design (Weeks 1–4)

1. **Establish the Student AI Leadership Club (Core Pod):**
   * Recruit 10–15 students passionate about educational technology, UI design, communications, and project management.
   * Designate 1 faculty advisor (Informatics, STEM, or Humanities teacher) as Academic Mentor (guiding pedagogy and ethical safety without micromanaging).
   * Define cross-functional roles: Training, Project Management, Prompt Engineering, UI Design, PR, and Logistics.

2. **Develop Formal Proposal & Pitch Before the School Board:**
   * Students construct a 5-tiered flexible budget plan (ranging from 6M to 19.5M VND).
   * Present proposal to school administrators to secure operational approval and event funding ([Download Pitching Template](../templates/Budget_Pitching_PR_Template_EN.md)).

3. **Needs Assessment & Challenge Standardization:**
   * Conduct school-wide student surveys to capture authentic learning obstacles (standardized test prep, math reasoning, science projects).
   * Develop a standardized challenge bank strictly enforcing: **AI must act as a Socratic hint-giver with full source citations, strictly refusing to solve problems directly**.

---

### 🔹 STAGE 2A: 4-Session Student-Led Workshop Series (Weeks 5–8)

The student core team directly facilitates interactive workshops for the broader student body:

* **Session 1: Foundational Mindset & Digital Safety:**
   * Deconstruct AI benefits versus passive cognitive offloading.
   * Sign the Zero-PII commitment: absolute prohibition against inputting personal data (names, IDs, passwords, phone numbers, faces) into AI.
   * Ratify the UNESCO-aligned Student AI Ethics Charter.
* **Session 2: Socratic AI & Smart Study Notebooks:**
   * Train peers to configure Gemini Notebooks anchored directly to official textbooks, eliminating generative hallucinations.
   * Practice the PARTS Socratic Prompting Framework to engineer AI as an inquiry partner.
* **Session 3: Hallucination Hunting & Natural Language App Building:**
   * Train learners to identify AI confabulations, flawed logic, and biased assumptions.
   * Introduce no-code "Vibe Coding" via Gemini Canvas to prototype interactive apps using natural language instructions.
* **Session 4: Prototype Polishing & Hackathon Sprint Prep:**
   * Guide 20 hackathon teams (80–100 students) in role specialization: Prompt Engineer, Content Auditor, UI Designer, and Lead Presenter.
   * Deliver functional MVPs ready for competitive evaluation.

---

### 🔹 STAGE 2B: School-Wide Safe AI Hackathon (Weeks 9–10)

1. **Event Scope & Scale:**
   * 80–100 student competitors across 20 teams, attended by teachers and parents.
   * 33 student committee members independently manage room assignments, tech support, timekeeping, and stage logistics.
2. **Contingency Management (Protocols BP-01 to BP-06):**
   * Dedicated offline hotspot failovers, backup demo accounts, and medical first-aid readiness.
3. **Core Deliverables:**
   * 20 safe learning assistants: SAT Prep Coach, Socratic Math Mentor, STEAM Advisor, Literature Analysis Companion.

---

### 🔹 STAGE 3: Multi-Stakeholder Evaluation & Sandbox Testing (Weeks 11–12)

1. **Live Sandbox Defense:**
   * Finalist teams demonstrate their tools before an interdisciplinary panel comprising faculty, tech professionals, and parent representatives.
   * Judges conduct live stress-tests for hallucination risks, answer-giving leaks, and Zero-PII compliance.
2. **Standardized 100-Point Evaluation:**
   * *65 points:* Socratic pedagogical fidelity (zero answer leaks) and data privacy safety.
   * *35 points:* Practical learning utility, intuitive UI, and student user experience.
   * ([View Scoring Rubric](05_AI_Appathon_Scorecard_Rubric_EN.md)).
3. **Iterative Refinement:**
   * Teams refine guardrails based on judge feedback prior to school-wide classroom integration.

---

## 🌐 SUSTAINABLE SCALING & INTER-SCHOOL TRANSFER

* **Semester 1 (Academic Year 2026–2027):** Pilot with core student leadership pod; secure school pitching approval; finalize open-source playbooks.
* **Semester 2 (Academic Year 2026–2027):** Execute school-wide workshop series and 20-team Safe AI Hackathon.
* **From Academic Year 2027–2028 Onward:** Package all resources as free Open Educational Resources (OER) under the MIT License for inter-school dissemination across national and global school networks.
