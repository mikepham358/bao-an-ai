# STUDENT CHARTER FOR SAFE, ETHICAL & ACADEMICALLY INTEGRAL AI USE
### (Aligned with UNESCO AI Competency Framework for Students 2024 & National Education Policies)

Every student participating in this framework voluntarily ratifies and practices four core safety principles:

---

### 1. ABSOLUTE PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (ZERO-PII)
* Never enter Personally Identifiable Information into AI models (full legal names, dates of birth, home addresses, phone numbers, passwords, bank accounts, or private biometric images).
* All assignments, research datasets, and coding experiments must use anonymous identifiers to protect self and peer privacy.

### 2. ACADEMIC INTEGRITY & TRANSPARENT ATTRIBUTION
* When utilizing AI for brainstorming, outlining, or linguistic refinement, students must proactively disclose how AI was consulted.
* Direct copy-pasting of AI-generated responses submitted as one's original work is strictly prohibited.
* Always cross-examine AI outputs against verified textbooks and authoritative primary literature; never accept generative outputs blindly.

### 3. AI FOR COGNITIVE ELEVATION, NEVER AS A WORK SHORTCUT
* Treat AI strictly as an **intellectual sparring partner** or **Socratic tutor** that fosters deep conceptual understanding through scaffolded inquiry.
* Resolutely reject the temptation to request instant answers. Boldly engage with challenging problems, embrace productive struggle, and iterate through trial and error.

### 4. NURTURING A SAFE & CIVIL DIGITAL SCHOOL ENVIRONMENT
* Never deploy AI to generate disinformation, malicious falsehoods, defamatory content, or inappropriate media contrary to school values.
* Actively guide and mentor fellow peers in responsible AI usage, fostering an inclusive, safe, and human-centered technology culture across the school.
