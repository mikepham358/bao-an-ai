# SAFE AI HACKATHON EVALUATION SCORECARD & RUBRIC
### (Standardized 100-Point Rubric for Multi-Stakeholder Judging Panels)

The evaluation panel—comprising classroom teachers, software engineers, and parent representatives—conducts live functional testing and scores 20 student finalist teams across four standardized dimensions:

---

| Evaluation Criteria | Scoring Focus | Max Points | Benchmark Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Socratic Pedagogy & Cognitive Elevation** | • Step-by-step scaffolded inquiry<br/>• Firm refusal to solve exercises outright<br/>• Encourages productive struggle and persistence | **30 pts** | When a user begs for direct answers, the AI tactfully declines and delivers a targeted hint to prompt self-reflection. |
| **2. Data Privacy & Anti-Hallucination Safety** | • Rigorous textbook citation anchoring<br/>• Zero tolerance for fabricated facts (anti-hallucination)<br/>• Absolute personal data protection (Zero-PII) | **35 pts** | Sources verified textbooks in Gemini Notebooks; flags immediate warnings if user accidentally inputs personal data. |
| **3. Authentic Curricular Utility** | • Directly resolves authentic student learning bottlenecks<br/>• High applicability across disciplines (Math, SAT, Science...) | **20 pts** | Demonstrates demonstrable learning gains and positive student feedback during empirical pilot tests. |
| **4. Usability & Sustainable Adoption** | • Intuitive, accessible UI across devices<br/>• Zero or minimal operating costs<br/>• Easily transferable to peer classrooms | **15 pts** | Clean and responsive user interface; clear documentation enabling effortless adoption by other classes. |
| **TOTAL** | **MULTI-STAKEHOLDER REVIEW PANEL** | **100 pts** | **Excellence Standard:** Total score ≥ 80 pts with ZERO safety violations in Dimension 2. |
